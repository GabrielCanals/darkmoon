Weapon (greatclub), rare (requires attunement)
This massive greatclub is carved from a single, dense stone,
imbued with afaint, resonating hum. Itfeels unusually heavy
and solid, evenfor its size. Legends whisper of this weapon
being carvedfrom the heart of a fallen mountain, its power
echoing the unyielding strength of the earth itself.

Stone Damage. The Monolith deals 2d6 bludgeoning damage
instead of Id8.

Monolith Endurance. While attuned to the Monolith, you
gain resistance to bludgeoning damage from nonmagical
weapons.

Charges. The Monolith has 3 charges. You can use only 1
charge per turn. You regain all expended charges at dawn.

Seismic Hit. Once per turn, when you hit a creature with the
Monolith, you can expend a charge to unleash a wave of
resonating force. Each creature within 10 feet of the target
(excluding you) must make a DC 15 Constitution saving
throw or be knocked prone.

Unstoppable Push. When you hit a creature or object with
the Monolith, you can expend a charge to push it up to 10
feet away from you in a straight line. If the creature or
object collides with a solid surface during this movement, it
takes an additional 1d6 bludgeoning damage.
