A note on an ancient corpse says “todays password is Loyalty”

The party comes to a door with a stone face, it begins speaking to them. The trick isn’t to say the password, but to convince the door to say it. The fun comes from roleplaying out the interaction.

The way it works is that the door only opens if the party convinces the door to say it.

So the party says “the password is loyalty” and the door says, “Why, yes, it is! How did you figure that out?” And then proceeds to not open.

My party said “Well, are you going to open?” And the door said “sorry, no.”
